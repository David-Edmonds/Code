# Job Opportunity Engine Blueprint

## Goal

Reduce time spent searching and preparing for low-fit roles while keeping every application accurate and personally reviewed.

## Suggested scoring

- Remote eligibility and location restrictions
- Salary at or above target
- Data analyst, BI, operations analytics, sales operations analytics, or adjacent role fit
- SQL, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, or reporting alignment
- Seniority match
- U.S. work authorization compatibility
- No clearance requirement that cannot be met
- Application age and expected competition

## Workflow

1. Gather roles from approved sources.
2. Normalize company, title, salary, location, date, and URL.
3. Deduplicate by company/title/source.
4. Score and explain the top matches.
5. David approves a role before resume tailoring or application preparation.
6. Save the exact resume version, answers, status, and follow-up date.

## Guardrail

Never submit an application, accept legal attestations, or answer experience questions without David's review.
